## What this project is

A companion library and preset catalog that maps game-facing archetype ids into Personality Engine constructor arguments. Numeric mappings labeled **project convention** are game-feel knobs, not psychometric scoring keys.

Cited authors have not endorsed this project or reviewed its coefficients.

## What this project is not

Do not represent Archetypes or its presets as:

- A diagnostic, personnel-selection, or high-stakes assessment tool
- Evidence for real-world group differences in intelligence or worth
- A substitute for licensed professional judgment

## Preset policy

The **public** catalog does not ship real-world race, ethnicity, or national cognitive rank presets. Fantasy clans, generic professions, and temperament climates use cited psychological **knobs** (cognitive stage, operant history, trait bands), not IQ or g-factor channels. Profession merge rules are in [`presets/README.md`](presets/README.md). Clan merge rules are in [`presets/clans/README.md`](presets/clans/README.md). Temperament merge rules are in [`presets/temperament/README.md`](presets/temperament/README.md).

Hosts may add private presets at their own risk and must obtain their own legal and ethical review.
